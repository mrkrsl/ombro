# Opening hours of three public libraries in Edinburgh

Done looks like: a Markdown file, workspace/libraries.md, with a short table
of three public libraries in Edinburgh, their weekday opening hours and the
page each figure came from.

Who uses it: someone planning a visit this week.

Binding constraint: every row has a source URL; nothing is guessed.

A waste would be: more than three libraries, or hours copied from a page
that does not state them.

Not wanted: university or private libraries; anything beyond opening hours.

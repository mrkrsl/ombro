# Two short updates for an invented community garden's channel

The writer drafts two posts, each under 280 characters: one on the new
compost bins, one on Saturday's open morning at 10am. The publisher then
posts each one, exactly as drafted, to the `garden-news` channel. (The
writer has no file tools, so the Lead passes the drafted text to the
publisher in its brief.)

Done looks like: two posts, staged for approval or posted, and listed in
the report.

Who uses it: the garden's members, reading the channel.

Binding constraint: under 280 characters each; nothing invented beyond the
two facts above.

A waste would be: posts that add dates, names or claims not given here.

Not wanted: hashtags, emoji, or a third post.

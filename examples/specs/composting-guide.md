# A one-page beginner's guide to home composting

Done looks like: about 300 words a first-time gardener can follow, with a
short list of what can and cannot go in the bin.

Who uses it: a community garden's newsletter, printed as-is.

Binding constraint: one page; no brand names.

A waste would be: anything longer than a page, or advice that needs special
equipment.

Not wanted: hot composting, wormeries, or anything about selling compost.
